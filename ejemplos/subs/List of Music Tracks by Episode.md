Each track is listed with its translated name, the soundtrack it originates from, a timestamp of when it is used in the episode, and a description of the corresponding scene.

**Note: Due to different interpretations and no official English spellings, keep in mind the titles used for the tracks below may be different than other translations. Please check the soundtrack pages for more clarification.**
